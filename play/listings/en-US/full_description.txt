Bolo is a bilingual video translation app for watching YouTube videos with a synced transcript and readable translation beside the video.

Paste a YouTube link, choose your languages, and Bolo prepares a timestamped transcript so you can watch, listen, and read in rhythm with the speaker.

Features:
• Synced video player: watch the video while the active transcript segment follows along.
• Bilingual reading: compare original speech with translated text in a calm, readable layout.
• Tap to seek: jump to a moment by tapping the transcript segment.
• Portrait and landscape support: make more room for the video or the translation when you need it.
• Saved history: return to videos you have already processed.
• Processing status: see when audio is being prepared, transcribed, translated, and cleaned up.
• Debug cost view: inspect Cloudflare and OpenRouter processing details when needed.

Bolo is designed for learners, families, and anyone who wants a quieter way to understand video in another language. It keeps the interface simple and lets the video and translation do the work.

Download Bolo to watch YouTube videos with synced bilingual captions, translated transcripts, and a reading experience that stays aligned with the audio.

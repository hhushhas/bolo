# Bolo Terms

Effective date: May 17, 2026

Bolo is provided as a video translation and transcript reading tool. Users are responsible for the YouTube URLs they submit and for complying with applicable laws, platform terms, and content rights.

Generated transcripts and translations may contain mistakes. Bolo should not be used as the sole source for legal, medical, financial, emergency, or other high-stakes decisions.

The service may fail when a video cannot be accessed, processed, transcribed, or translated. Availability depends on third-party services including YouTube, Convex, Cloudflare, and OpenRouter.

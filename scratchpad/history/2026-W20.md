# Weekly history: 2026-W20

Period: 2026-05-11 to 2026-05-17

## Sources

- `scratchpad/video-player-rehaul-session-log-2026-05-16.md`

## Decisions and outcomes

- Bolo moved to a synced bilingual video-player contract: temporary media is prepared in a worker/container, transcription and translation run as durable workflow steps, and the app presents timestamped bilingual segments with seek and follow behavior.
- The workflow gained chunk cleanup, retry and resume handling, usage accounting, and fixes for long-video failures, provider response shape changes, and YouTube media access. Real smoke jobs reached ready and temporary chunks were deleted.

## Risks and open points

- The original rollout had container, YouTube, and provider failure modes. Later hardening covered the observed cases, but future provider or YouTube changes remain operational risks.
